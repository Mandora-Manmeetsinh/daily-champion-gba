Daily Champion
Daily Champion is an unofficial fan project. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nintendo, Creatures Inc., GAME FREAK inc. or The Pokémon Company. Pokémon, character names and sprites belong to their owners. Sprites come from the PokeAPI sprites collection. Battles use a simplified custom engine, not Pokémon Showdown. Champion teams are fan-simplified rosters of six unique Pokémon, so they won't match every in-game team exactly.
